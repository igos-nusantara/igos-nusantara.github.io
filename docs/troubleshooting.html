Troubleshooting

Common problems and what to do about them.

The computer does not start from the USB stick

  • Open the boot menu with the right key (often F12, F11, F9 or Esc) and pick the stick by hand.
  • Try another USB port, preferably one directly on the computer.
  • Write the stick again. On Windows, use DD Image mode in Rufus, or use balenaEtcher.

The screen stays black

Restart and choose Start IGOS Nusantara 26 (basic graphics) in the boot menu.

Starting stops partway

In the boot menu, open Troubleshooting and choose the verbose entry. The last lines on screen show where it stops. If your computer has Broadcom Wi-Fi, try the entry without the Broadcom wl driver.

Nabau says the installation medium is damaged

Part of the USB stick cannot be read as it was written. Nothing on your disks has changed. Check the image's SHA-256 (see Download), write it to the stick again, or use another stick. If you used Ventoy, try writing the image directly with Fedora Media Writer, Rufus or balenaEtcher.

Wi-Fi does not work

Open Drivers & Hardware in Settings: if your card needs firmware that is not installed, it is under Recommended. See Drivers & Hardware.

Check that your card or adapter is listed in Hardware, and try the nightly image, which has more drivers. If it still does not work, report a bug and include the output of lspci -nn and lsusb.

Android apps could not be turned on

Usually the 1.3 GB download stopped. Run sudo lapis init -type VANILLA in Terminal; it continues where it stopped. See Android apps.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue at github.com/igos-nusantara/bayu/issues and choose Laporan Bug. The form is in Indonesian, and you may answer in English. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and attach a screenshot if you can. For a hardware problem, paste the information from Copy Hardware Information (Settings > Drivers & Hardware).
